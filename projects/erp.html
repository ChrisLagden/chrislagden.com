Overview

The challenge

BOB2 was scaling rapidly but operating on a fragmented patchwork of spreadsheets, manual processes, and disconnected tools. Order management, stock control, supplier purchasing, and financial reporting all lived in separate systems — creating data silos, manual re-entry, and a growing operational debt that was limiting the business.

The brief was to design and implement a unified ERP solution that could handle the full order lifecycle, provide real-time visibility across the business, and support continued growth without adding headcount.

Approach

How we got there

Starting with a full discovery phase — stakeholder interviews, process mapping, and a technical audit of existing systems — I built a clear picture of where data was being lost and where manual effort was highest.

From there I scoped and wireframed the ERP architecture, working through each business domain: purchasing, stock management, order processing, fulfilment, and reporting. I led stakeholder workshops to validate the proposed flows before a single line of code was written.

Implementation was phased to minimise disruption. Third-party logistics and courier APIs were integrated first to stabilise fulfilment, followed by the stock and purchasing modules, then reporting dashboards built in Power BI sitting on top of the unified data layer.

Deliverables

What was built

  • End-to-end ERP platform covering order management, stock control, purchasing, and fulfilment
  • Custom Power BI dashboards giving real-time visibility on sales, margins, and stock levels
  • Automated reporting workflows reducing manual reconciliation time by ~80%
  • Third-party logistics API integrations with major UK courier services
  • Supplier purchasing module with automated PO generation based on reorder thresholds
  • Process documentation and staff training materials
